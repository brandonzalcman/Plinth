# Plinth 0.1.1

Plinth now has a Settings window, and a few 0.1.0 features that had never been tried by hand are checked and fixed.

## Highlights

- **Settings** (Plinth › Settings…, ⌘,). A window styled like Plinth, with sections for:
  - **General:** make Plinth your default browser, and choose your search engine: Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Ecosia, Brave Search, or your own URL.
  - **Privacy & security:** ad and tracker blocking, third-party cookie blocking, always use HTTPS (asks before opening a site that has no secure version), Global Privacy Control, and Clear Browsing Data.
  - **Downloads:** choose the folder, ask where to save each file, and open safe files after downloading.
  - **Startup & pages:** restore your last session or start fresh, and choose what a new page shows or set a homepage.
  - **Appearance & bar:** accent colour, how long pages stay on the bar, default page zoom, and font sizes.
  - **On-device AI:** the master switch, plus separate switches for search by meaning and page summaries.
  - **Import:** bring bookmarks and history from Safari, Chrome, Arc and Zen. Plinth has no bookmark folders, so bookmarks are added to the shelf, grouped by site. Arc has no bookmarks file, so its saved sidebar tabs are imported instead; Zen's pinned tabs are not. Safari needs Full Disk Access for Plinth.
- **Fixed:** Ask About This Page (⌘J) no longer acts on a hidden window when a mini window is in front.

## Good to know

- Content blocking and cookie changes are fully applied after a page reloads. Font-size changes affect text sized relative to the page, not fixed pixel sizes.
- Switching "Fade pages to the shelf" re-checks the bar straight away and can put pages away.

Requires macOS 26. The AI features need a Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on.
