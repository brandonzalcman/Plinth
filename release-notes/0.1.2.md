# Plinth 0.1.2

Plinth now has its own password manager, a dark mode, a welcome guide, smarter address-bar suggestions, a History view, and a downloads tray.

## Highlights

- **Back, forward and reload.** Buttons at the left of the bar. Back and forward grey out when there's nowhere to go, and reload turns into stop while a page loads. ⌘[, ⌘] and ⌘R still work.
- **Passwords.** Plinth offers to save logins and fills them in on later visits, stored in your Mac's Keychain, with no Apple Passwords app needed. Manage them in Settings › Passwords (Touch ID to reveal or copy). Logins only fill on the exact site they were saved for.
  - **Import** saved passwords from Chrome, Arc and Zen, or from a CSV. Safari doesn't allow other apps to read its passwords, so export a CSV from Safari instead. Chrome and Arc ask for Keychain access. Zen can't be imported if it has a primary password set.
- **Dark mode.** Settings › Appearance & bar has System, Light and Dark. The bar, shelf, panels and Settings all follow it. Heads up: with System, the bar now follows your Mac rather than each page's colour.
- **Welcome guide.** The first time you open Plinth you get a short animation and a four-card guide to the bar, the command bar, the shelf and search by meaning, and importing. Replay it any time from Plinth › Show Welcome Guide. Existing users aren't shown it.
- **Smarter address bar.** Suggestions put the page you most likely want first, collapse the many near-duplicate links to the same page, show at most two history rows per site, and learn from what you pick.
- **History.** ⌘Y opens a History panel: search, filter by Today / Yesterday / 7 days / 30 days / All or by site, grouped by day, with repeat visits merged.
- **Edit the address from the bar.** Click the page you're on to open its address with the URL selected, ready to change.
- **Sign-in popups work.** Pages that open a sign-in or payment window ("Sign in with Google" and similar) now get a real popup window that can talk back to the page, instead of opening a second, disconnected page.
- **Shelf: Clear all.** Clear everything on the shelf at once, with Undo for 15 seconds.
- **Downloads tray.** A button beside the shelf shows download progress, speed and time left; open a file, show it in Finder, cancel, or clear the list.
- **Smoother.** Less work on every layout pass of the bar, while dragging the split divider, and when opening the shelf.
- **Versioned downloads.** The disk image is now named after its version, for example `Plinth-0.1.2.dmg`. The usual download link still always gets the newest version.
- **Version in Settings.** Settings › General has an About section showing the version and build number, with **Check for Updates…**.

## Good to know

- Passwords: sign-ins in two steps (username on one page, password on the next) and logins inside frames aren't captured yet.
- Downloads can be cancelled but not paused.
- Plinth updates itself in the background; the new version applies the next time you reopen it.

Requires macOS 26. The AI features need a Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on.
