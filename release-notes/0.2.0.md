# Plinth 0.2.0

Plinth now has bookmarks, private windows, sites that fold down to their icon, and iCloud sync you can turn on.

## Highlights

- **Bookmarks.** ⌘D saves the page you're on and shows a small card where you can rename it, pick a folder or remove it. ⌥⌘B opens the Bookmarks panel: search, folders, rename, move and remove. Bookmarks also show up in the command bar under their own heading.
  - **Imported bookmarks keep their folders.** Importing from Safari, Chrome, Arc or Zen now puts bookmarks in Bookmarks under "Imported from …" instead of spreading them across the shelf.
- **Private window.** File › New Private Window (⇧⌘N) opens a separate, dark-tinted window with a Private badge. It keeps no history, shelf entries, resume cards or search index, and its cookies and site data are thrown away when you close it. Private pages aren't restored at the next launch and aren't synced.
- **Fold a site.** Click the icon at the head of a site with several pages to fold it down to just the icon, with a count of its pages. Click again to open it back out. Going to any of its pages another way opens it too.
- **iCloud sync.** Plinth › iCloud Sync turns on syncing of pins, the shelf and history between your Macs, plus a read-only list of each Mac's open pages. It's off by default and uses your own private iCloud storage. This is the first release that can sync, so treat it as new.
- **Search suggestions you can see.** As you type a search, suggestions from your default search engine now appear as their own rows, with the part that continues what you typed in bold. Before, they looked like repeats of "Search for …". Private windows don't ask for them.
- **Click the pin you're on to edit its address.** Clicking a pinned site that's already active opens the address, the same as clicking the page you're on.
- **Groups work when you press Group.** Pressing Group on a suggestion now moves you onto a page in the group, so the other sites actually leave the bar. Before, the site you were on stayed put and nothing seemed to happen.
- **Clearing history reaches your other Macs.** With sync on, Settings › Privacy › Clear browsing data now removes the cleared history everywhere, so it doesn't come back.

## Good to know

- Bookmarks don't sync between Macs yet.
- Inside a private window there are no pins, no shelf and no reopen closed page. Files you download there stay on disk; only their rows in the downloads tray go when the window closes.
- With sync on, clearing a time range (last hour, today) can miss a page another Mac hasn't uploaded yet. Clearing all time has no such gap.
- Passkeys and Apple Passwords autofill still aren't available. They need an entitlement from Apple, which has been requested.

Requires macOS 26. The AI features need a Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on.
