# Plinth 0.2.1

Plinth now has pin folders, points and levels that unlock new icons and colours, suggested pages when you open the search bar, and password saving that works on far more sign-in pages.

## Highlights

- **Pin folders.** Right-click a pinned site and choose Move to Folder to group pins under one tile. A folder takes one tile's width however many pins it holds, and shows a small mosaic of their icons. Click it to open a tray of its pins; right-click it to rename or ungroup. Folders sync with your pins when iCloud Sync is on.
- **Points & Levels.** Plinth now gives points for using it: for each day you use it (more on a streak), for sharing your level, for trying a feature for the first time, and a bonus for using it on both Mac and iPhone. Six levels, from Footing to Cornerstone, each unlock an app icon and a matching accent colour. See yours in Settings › Points & Levels. Points stay on your devices, and with iCloud Sync on they add up across them. Private windows earn nothing.
  - **App icons.** Settings › Appearance & Bar has six icons in the brand colours: Sunrise, Dusk, Meadow, Ember, Midnight and Mono. The icon you pick shows in the Dock while Plinth is running.
  - **Brand accent colours.** The colour of each level you reach can be used for the current page, buttons and switches, in light and dark.
  - **Share card.** Share your level as an image with your badge and points. It shows no pages or sites.
- **Suggested pages.** Open the search bar with nothing typed and Plinth lists the pages you go back to most, above the ones you recently put away. With iCloud Sync on, that includes pages you visited on your other devices.
- **Passwords work on many more sites.** Plinth now finds sign-in forms that appear late, have no form around them, sit inside a frame, or ask for your user name and password on separate steps, and offers to fill and save there. Sign-up and change-password forms are no longer filled by mistake, a wrong password no longer prompts you to save, and a sign-in with a slow two-factor step still offers to save. A login saved for `example.com` also fills on `www.example.com`.
- **Passkeys.** Sign in and create passkeys on websites, using the passkeys in your iCloud Keychain or a security key. This is the first release with passkeys, so treat it as new.
- **Faster resume cards.** The "where you left off" card now takes one step to write, so it appears sooner.
- **A first page.** A brand-new Plinth opens getplinth.dev as its first page, once. Typing "plinth" in the search bar suggests it too.

## Fixes

- Search suggestions stay quick with a very long history, and keep up after history arrives from or is cleared on another device.

## Good to know

- A chosen app icon shows only while Plinth is running. The icon in Finder and the Applications folder stays Sunrise, and the Dock goes back to it when Plinth quits.
- Changing your Mac's clock can earn extra days of points. Points are for fun, so Plinth doesn't police it.
- With iCloud Sync on, pin folders and points reach your other devices once those run 0.2.1 or later. A Mac still on 0.2.0 keeps syncing pins and simply doesn't show folders.
- Pins are grouped from the right-click menu; dragging one pin onto another doesn't make a folder yet.

Requires macOS 26. The AI features need a Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on.
